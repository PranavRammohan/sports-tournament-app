Delete your PlayMySet account

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

Open PlayMySet → Profile → Delete Account, enter your password to confirm, and your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@playmyset.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm and process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your name, email address, phone number, and profile picture are removed and your account can no longer be logged into. Match results you played in are kept in an anonymized form (shown as "Deleted user") so that other players' tournament history, standings, and ratings stay accurate — see our Privacy Policy for details.
