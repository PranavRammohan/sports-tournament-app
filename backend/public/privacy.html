PlayMySet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

PlayMySet ("we", "us") is a sports league and tournament management app for racquet sports (badminton, tennis, table tennis, pickleball). This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Gender
  • Location / area (city or region you enter)
  • A password (stored only as a salted hash — we cannot see or recover it)
  • An optional profile picture

As you use the app, we also store:

  • Tournaments you join or host, and your role in them
  • Match results you report or that are reported for you, including optional photos attached to a match
  • A skill rating per sport, calculated from your match results
  • In-app notifications

How your phone number is shared

Your phone number is not visible to every member of a tournament. It is shown only to the specific opponent(s) you are scheduled to play against in a given fixture, so you can coordinate a time and place to play. It is never shown to players you are not scheduled against, and it is never sold or shared with anyone outside the app.

How we use this information

We use your information solely to operate the app: running tournaments, generating schedules, calculating ratings and standings, sending you in-app and email notifications about your matches and account, and letting you coordinate matches with your scheduled opponents.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Match results you were involved in are retained even after other players delete their accounts, so tournament history and standings stay accurate for everyone else who played in them.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app, or by visiting our account deletion page if you no longer have the app installed.

Deleting your account removes your personal identifying information (name, email, phone number, profile picture) and prevents anyone from logging into that account again. Match results you played in are kept in an anonymized form (shown as "Deleted user") so that other players' tournament history, standings, and ratings — which depend on those results — remain accurate. This is the same reason most tournament and league platforms retain match history after an account is closed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@playmyset.app.
